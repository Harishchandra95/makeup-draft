About Pranali

I do bridal, party and photoshoot makeup in Mumbai. My approach is simple: look after the skin, match the tone exactly, and keep the finish light enough that you can still move your face. Most clients tell me they look like themselves, only rested.

Bring photos of looks you like and tell me what you don't want. I will tell you what suits your skin and the lighting at your event.

  • Skin first

    A short skin prep before every look, so makeup sits well and lasts.

  • Shade matching

    Base matched in daylight, so your face and neck agree.

  • Clean kit

    Brushes sanitised between clients, single-use applicators for lips and lashes.

  • Touch-up ready

    Bridal bookings include a small touch-up kit for the day.
